Just little by little, I started with a grammar with only 
defun and comp:

%import common.CNAME
%import common.WS

%ignore WS

program : defun+

defun   : CNAME ":" funspec

funspec : CNAME                            -> single
        | "comp" funspec funspec           -> comp

0/ Acknowledgement: Huge help received from Gemini along 
many discussions of the options to handle the Lark 
Transformer and the choice of the best way.

1/ The v_args decorator allows the methods in the Transformer
instance to avoid the "children" tuple and refer directly
to the subtree funspec's. Can be used on each method or
globally for the class (but it is a bit noisy for the 
very first rule). Gemini said that it also works with 
Visitor but in a subtly different way: it unpacks the 
unmodified subtrees instead of the modified ones coming 
back from the recursion as does for Transformer instances.
However, Gemini also said that Visitor works top-down and,
a couple of days later, was saying the contrary on both
accounts.
